Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 15, 2026 | GDPR & COPPA Compliant

1. Introduction

Young Innovators ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of all users, especially children. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information when you use our educational platform.

We comply with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) (EU) 2016/679 and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) (15 U.S.C. §§ 6501-6506).

2. Data Controller

Young Innovators Educational Platform
123 Innovation Street, San Francisco, CA 94102
Data Protection Officer: dpo@younginnovators.com
Privacy Inquiries: privacy@younginnovators.com

3. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Special Protections for Children Under 13:

  • We NEVER collect personal information from children without verifiable parental consent
  • Student accounts can ONLY be created by verified parents/guardians
  • Children's data is NEVER used for marketing, advertising, or profiling
  • Children CANNOT make their information publicly available
  • We collect ONLY the minimum data necessary for educational services
  • Parents can review, modify, or delete their child's data at ANY TIME
  • No third-party sharing of children's data without explicit parental consent

3.1 Verifiable Parental Consent

Before collecting any information from a child, we require verifiable parental consent through:

  • Parent account verification (email + phone)
  • Explicit consent checkbox with detailed data collection disclosure
  • Consent records maintained with timestamps

4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR Article 6)

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) - For children's data, with verifiable parental consent
  • Performance of Contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) - To provide the educational service
  • Legitimate Interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) - Platform security, fraud prevention

5. Your Rights (GDPR Articles 15-22)

  • Right of Access (Art. 15) - Request a copy of all your data
  • Right to Rectification (Art. 16) - Correct inaccurate data
  • Right to Erasure (Art. 17) - Request deletion ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to Restrict Processing (Art. 18) - Limit data usage
  • Right to Data Portability (Art. 20) - Export in machine-readable format
  • Right to Object (Art. 21) - Object to processing
  • Right to Withdraw Consent - At any time, without affecting prior processing

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@younginnovators.com. We will respond within 30 days.

6. Data Security Measures

  • TLS/HTTPS encryption for all data in transit
  • AES-256 encryption for data at rest
  • Passwords hashed with bcrypt (never stored in plaintext)
  • Regular security audits and penetration testing
  • Principle of least privilege for data access
  • Automatic session timeout (30 minutes inactivity)
  • Rate limiting on authentication endpoints
  • Input validation and sanitization (XSS/SQL injection prevention)
  • CSRF token protection on all forms
  • Content Security Policy (CSP) headers

7. Cookies & Tracking

We use only essential and preference cookies. We do NOT use:

  • Third-party advertising cookies
  • Cross-site tracking
  • Behavioral profiling of children
  • Social media tracking pixels

8. Data Retention

  • Active accounts: Data retained while account is active
  • Deleted accounts: All data permanently removed within 30 days
  • Children's data: Reviewed annually; deleted if account inactive for 12 months
  • Consent records: Retained for 3 years for legal compliance

9. Third-Party Sharing

We do NOT sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. Data may only be shared with:

  • Service providers (hosting, security) under strict data processing agreements
  • Law enforcement when legally required

10. International Data Transfers

If data is transferred outside the EU/EEA, we ensure adequate protection through Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) or adequacy decisions.

11. Data Breach Notification

In the event of a data breach, we will:

  • Notify the relevant supervisory authority within 72 hours (GDPR Art. 33)
  • Notify affected users without undue delay if high risk (GDPR Art. 34)
  • Document the breach and remediation steps
